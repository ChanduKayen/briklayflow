Be honest about it

Where this works, and where it stops.

AI is good at tools where numbers go in and an answer comes out. It is weak where data must be saved and shared by many people every day.

Ask for a phone tool

  • Opens from a link, like an app
  • Customers and staff can use it
  • Big buttons, nothing to break

Ask for Excel or a Google Sheet

  • You see and change every formula
  • Right for lists and ledgers that grow
  • A Dashboard tab can sit on top

Every prompt here can be copied either way. You choose on the last step.

Build yourself

  • Price estimators
  • Quote makers
  • Daily calculators
  • Checklists and reminders

Keep buying

  • Accounts and GST billing
  • Inventory
  • Tools many staff share
  1. AThe rates in these prompts are samples. Every prompt tells the AI to confirm each number with you. Give it your real ones.
  2. BOnly share numbers you are happy to make public. Your competitors can open the same link your customers do.
  3. CCannot find the Share button? Ask the AI: "How do I share this as a link?" It will tell you where it is.
